About Us

Born from Rescue. Driven by Longevity.

Gentle Giants Dog Food and Products wasn't created in a corporate boardroom—it was born from a lifelong mission of rescue, love, and dedication to animals. Founded by Burt Ward (famous worldwide as "Robin" from the classic Batman series) and his wife Tracy Ward, our journey began out of a desperate need to save the lives of giant and large-breed dogs that were suffering from poor health and tragically short lifespans.

After rescuing thousands of dogs and realizing that commercial pet foods were failing them, Burt and Tracy spent years working with top nutritional experts, veterinarians, and scientists. Their goal was simple yet revolutionary: create a food that would help dogs live the longest, happiest, and healthiest lives possible.

The benefits of Gentle Giants

Six reasons vets and pet parents choose Gentle Giants for dogs of every size and age.

01

Non-GMO and Clean Ingredients

Recipes avoid GMOs, meat by-products, corn, wheat, soy, and artificial colors or preservatives.

02

Heart-Healthy Fat Levels

Dry formulas feature a lower fat content (around 9% minimum) and moderate protein (22% to 24%) to reduce cardiovascular strain.

03

Digestive Support

Added prebiotics and probiotics, along with fiber-rich grains like oatmeal and brown rice, help dogs absorb nutrients and maintain firm stools.

04

Hip and Joint Care

Formulas include glucosamine, chondroitin, or New Zealand green lipped mussels to protect bones and cartilage, which is especially helpful for large breeds.

05

Skin, Coat, and Immune Health

Guaranteed omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids nourish the skin and coat, while whole fruits and vegetables like blueberries, cranberries, spinach, and sweet potatoes provide natural antioxidants.

06

All-Life-Stage Versatility

Both dry and canned options meet nutritional standards for puppies, adults, and seniors of any size, letting multi-dog households share one food.

Our Difference
